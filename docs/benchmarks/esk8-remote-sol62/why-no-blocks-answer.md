I did **not** try `block` placement or automatic `wire_groups` during the task. I assumed that explicit geometry would give me more control over branch order, ground returns, and label placement on the dense sheets. I had no comparison to show it would take less work. Trying the guide’s approach on a capacitor bank or pull-up group would have given me that evidence. What I observed was specific to the manual approach: one explicit crossing was rejected, and rendered close-ups revealed text touching symbol artwork despite a zero-overlap report.

I’d rely on automation for these ICs if I could fix the IC and surrounding blocks, specify where connected parts should sit relative to particular pins, and preserve local wiring relationships such as a feedback tap on the output side of an inductor. The preview should show which groups it wired continuously or split with labels, flag crossings and clearance problems against actual rendered ink, and explain where it could not satisfy a placement or wiring constraint. I’d start with a single local group and inspect the result before using it across a page.
